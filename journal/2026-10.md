**Amended (evidence: IARV T2 on Aug 31; −15% tier fired with nothing tradeable on Sep 28):**
1. **Dislocation legs are tier-scaled, and thin high-beta names are second-tier-only.** The first (−5%) tier deploys *half* the intended slice, the −10% tier the next ~third, the −15% the remainder; a name that dropped ≥10% intraday on the dislocation day with BET-beta ≥1.5 (IARV-type) is not bought in the *first* tier. This turns a "fires once at full size" rule into a ladder, matching how BVB corrections actually unfold (Aug 31 −7% → Sep 28 −15%).
2. **Every active candidate carries a written tier-conditioned band** (the weekly plan must state, per name, the price at which it is a buy *if BET is ≥10% / ≥15% off ATH* — a market-wide discount legitimises a valuation haircut — in addition to its valuation band). Rationale: the −15% tier fired on Sep 28 and the engine could not deploy a single RON, though that was the exact event it was preparing for (counterfactual §4).
3. **A8/A12-style "falsification-rule" assumptions are encouraged** (pre-committed fail conditions produced clean grades); timing assumptions on physical/state/political processes (A9, A4) must carry **an explicit buffer of ≥2 weeks** and are graded "unclear" by default if the underlying event is scheduled within 3 days of check-by.
No change to caps, bands' valuation anchors or the −5/−10/−15 levels. This is an evidence-based refinement of **sizing and plan-writing**, not a change of stance; the one-sample caveat is stated: the IARV loss may be pure beta (market) and the ladder costs nothing if the market rebounds only in one leg.
