## 3. Fiscal & politics
Fiscal:
- **2025 final ESA deficit: 7.9% of GDP** (Eurostat, published Apr 22, 2026) — beat the 8.4% revised target; largest fiscal correction in the EU (from a 2024 deficit **revised up to 9.3%**, vs. 8.6% previously known) ([Romania Insider](https://www.romania-insider.com/romania-public-deficit-january-2026); [Business Forum, 2026-04-22](https://www.businessforum.ro/finance/20260422/romania-achieves-largest-eu-fiscal-correction-in-2025-3116)). Cash deficit 2025: 7.65% of GDP (MinFin, Jan 2026).
- **2026 trajectory: on track so far.** Q1 cash deficit 1.0% of GDP (halved y/y); **H1 2026 cash deficit 2.0% of GDP, -41% y/y** ([Romania Insider H1](https://www.romania-insider.com/romania-public-deficit-h1-2026); [Q1](https://www.romania-insider.com/romania-public-deficit-q1-2026)). Targets: **6.0% ESA / 6.2% cash for 2026** (2.4pp consolidation) ([Romania Insider](https://www.romania-insider.com/romania-fiscal-consolidation-target-2026)); Fitch estimates 5.9%, Moody's 6.3%.
- **EDP: major de-escalation.** European Commission Spring Package (Jun 3, 2026): Romania "has taken effective action"; "no further action... necessary at this stage" — reversing the June 2025 "no effective action" finding that had triggered fund-suspension mechanics ([Romania Insider](https://www.romania-insider.com/eu-commission-romania-effective-action-deficit-june-2026); [EC EDP overview](https://economy-finance.ec.europa.eu/economic-governance-framework/stability-and-growth-pact/corrective-arm-excessive-deficit-procedure/excessive-deficit-procedures-overview_en)).
- **PNRR (RRF): crunch time.** Plan worth EUR 21.41bn (13.57 grants + 7.84 loans). 4th payment (EUR 2.62bn) cleared by EU committee June 2026 → ~EUR 12.97bn received (~61% absorption). **~EUR 4.5bn of grants still hinge on 6 essential laws; ALL milestones due Aug 31, 2026; final payment request due Sep 30, 2026.** Caretaker PM Bolojan requested an extraordinary parliamentary session in July to pass PNRR laws; EUR 6.3bn of projects flagged at risk of missing the deadline ([Romania Insider — 4th payment](https://www.romania-insider.com/eu-committee-fourth-pnrr-request-june-2026), [6 laws](https://www.romania-insider.com/romania-pm-six-laws-pnrr-july-2026), [extraordinary session](https://www.romania-insider.com/bolojan-extraordinary-parliament-meeting-pnrr-july-2026); [Romania Journal](https://www.romaniajournal.ro/politics/e6-3-billion-pnrr-projects-at-risk-of-missing-2026-deadline/); [OSW, 2026-07-14](https://www.osw.waw.pl/en/publikacje/analyses/2026-07-14/romania-no-closer-to-a-new-government-political-deadlock-and-risk)).

## Bottom line for the engine
1. **Rates stay high for longer** — no NBR easing until ~mid-2027 per consensus; the "May 2026 cut" thesis is dead. Carry on cash (5.5% deposit facility floor) remains decent; equity valuations have rallied anyway.
2. **Disinflation resumes from here** (10.85% May peak → NBR 5.5% Dec-2026): the July CPI print (mid-Aug) is the pivotal data point.
3. **Fiscal is the bright spot** (7.9% 2025, ~2.0% H1-2026 cash, EDP "effective action", all three agencies affirmed) — **politics is the dark spot** (caretaker government since May 5, two failed PM attempts, PNRR deadline Aug 31).
4. **No MSCI EM catalyst until at least June 2027**; FTSE SNP re-inclusion earliest Sep 2027.
5. **Market regime: full-blown momentum melt-up at ATHs on record volumes, decoupled from the macro.** BET +47.9% YTD. The engine should respect the trend but assume elevated drawdown risk into the Aug 15–Sep 30 political/PNRR window and the Sep/Oct rating reviews; the May episode showed drawdowns get bought within hours.
