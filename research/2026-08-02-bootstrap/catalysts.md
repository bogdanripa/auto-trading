## 4. Government / state actions on listed companies

- **Electricity**: price caps ended Jul 1, 2025. Support is now a 50 RON/month voucher for vulnerable consumers (~710k beneficiaries), **extended through Dec 31, 2026** (Ministry of Labor announcement; Digi24/ProTV, 2026). No re-cap on the table as of research date → H2O/SNN/EL sell at market.
- **Gas**: household cap regime replaced. **OUG 12/2026: Apr 1, 2026 – Mar 31, 2027**, producers (SNP, SNG) must deliver domestic-production gas for household consumption + cogeneration heat at **110 RON/MWh**; household invoice = min(contract price, formula with supply margin ≤15 RON/MWh) (News.ro/Financial Intelligence, Feb 2026; in force). Expiry Mar 31, 2027 = a 2027 catalyst; watch for extension debate starting late 2026.
- **Windfall tax (Energy Transition Fund contribution)**: CCR ruled the 100% over-450(400) RON/MWh clawback unconstitutional (Dec 2024 decision); **OUG 33/2025** retroactively set it at 80% for Sep 2022–Mar 2025; state collected ~13–20bn RON; ~1.5bn RON compensations to over-taxed producers budgeted (cursdeguvernare/Profit.ro). Litigation ongoing → possible one-off refunds for H2O/SNN (timing unverifiable). No FTE contribution found applying to current 2026 sales.
- **Bank tax**: turnover tax (ICA) at **4% continues through 2026** (raised from 2% as of Jul 1, 2025; small banks 2%); banks expected to pay ~2.7bn RON ICA in 2026 (ZF/Economica). Direct drag on TLV/BRD earnings — already in run-rate.
- **Grid tariffs**: ANRE Orders 73 & 74/Dec 16, 2025: from **Jan 1, 2026** Transelectrica system-services tariff +14.93% (14.70 RON/MWh), transmission tariffs +10.3% (TG 3.63, TL 36.45 RON/MWh) — supports TEL 2026 earnings. (Agerpres Dec 19, 2025.) No mid-year TEL/TGN tariff decision found scheduled for H2 2026; next regular reset Jan 1, 2027 (watch ANRE consultations in Q4).
- **State selling / new listings (supply events)**: Government note from vice-PM Oana Gheorghiu (Bursa.ro Apr 16 & 23, 2026): priorities = **ABB of 5–10% of Hidroelectrica (3.1–6.2bn RON) recommended for Q2–Q3 2026** (state to keep ≥70%), **Romgaz 5–7% (2.2–3.1bn RON)**, **CEC Bank IPO** (possibly 2026); ~22 SOEs identified, 3–8bn RON targeted for 2026. Salrom IPO pushed to ~Q1 2027 (Praid audit), Aeroporturi București 2027–28 (FP litigation). **An H2O/SNG placement in Sep–Dec 2026 is the single biggest known supply-overhang risk for those two names.**
