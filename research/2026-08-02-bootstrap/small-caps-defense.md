## 2. DEFENSE THEME — the highest-conviction structural story

**Macro:** Romania signed its SAFE (Security Action for Europe) agreement with the European Commission on **21 May 2026** for **EUR 16.68 bn (2026–2030), 2nd-largest allocation in the EU after Poland** (45-yr maturity loans, 10-yr grace, ≤3% rate). ~EUR 4.2 bn goes to dual-use A7/A8 highways, the rest to military equipment; MApN proposed **21 equipment projects ≈ EUR 9.6 bn** through SAFE; the government committed that **~50% of SAFE-funded product value be produced in Romania**; industry association ROMDEF formed. Sources: [MFE gov release](https://mfe.gov.ro/romania-primeste-166-miliarde-de-euro-prin-security-action-for-europe-safe-pentru-aparare-si-infrastructura-rutiera/), [Financiarul](https://financiarul.ro/companii/finantare-europeana-de-1668-miliarde-de-euro-pentru-apararea-romaniei-consolidare-in-sectorul-de-securitate/), [MApN SAFE page](https://www.mapn.ro/programul_safe/index.php), [gov.ro status](https://gov.ro/ro/stiri/stadiul-implementarii-programului-safe). Defense rallies have been broad: in one March 2026 session ARS +10%, IARV +12%, AVIO +17%, TBM +7% ([ZF](https://www.zf.ro/burse-fonduri-mutuale/bursa-planul-inarmare-nivelul-ue-salta-actiunile-industria-aparare-22724450)).
